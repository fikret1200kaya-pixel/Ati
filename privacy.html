Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

Waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we store only your email address. We use it to tell you when ATİ launches and for nothing else. We never sell or share it. Reply to any email or write to us to be removed at any time.

The ATİ app

  • Your notes, reminders, routines and settings are stored on your own computer.
  • The “Hey ATİ” wake word is recognised on your PC. Audio is not sent anywhere for wake-word detection.
  • Screen and audio recordings are saved only to the folder you choose; ATİ never uploads them.
  • When you ask ATİ an open question, your message is sent to the AI provider you selected in settings (for example OpenAI, Anthropic or Google), under that provider's privacy terms. Simple commands such as opening apps or setting reminders are handled locally.
  • Live information (weather, exchange rates, news, Wikipedia) is fetched only when you ask for it.
