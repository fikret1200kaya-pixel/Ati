Island Dock — Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

Island Dock is a Mac-style Dock and Dynamic Island–style bar for Windows. It has no account, no ads and no analytics. We (EFKA Ajans) do not collect, store or sell any personal data.

What stays on your PC

  • Your Dock items, settings, notes, to-do list, shelf files and pinned clipboard items are stored only on your computer.
  • Clipboard history is kept in memory while the app runs and is never uploaded. Items that password managers mark as private are not recorded.
  • Notifications are read locally (only after you allow it in Windows) to show them in the island and as Dock badges. They are never sent anywhere.
  • The microphone/camera indicator only checks whether another app is using them; Island Dock never accesses your microphone or camera itself.
  • Screen recording uses Windows' built-in recorder (Xbox Game Bar); recordings are saved to your Videos folder by Windows.

Internet services (only for the features you use)

  • Weather: the city you type is sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to get the temperature.
  • Lyrics: the title and artist of the song playing are sent to LRCLIB (lrclib.net) to fetch synced lyrics. You can turn lyrics off in Settings.
  • Calendar: if you add an iCal link, the app downloads that calendar directly from your provider (for example Google or Outlook).
  • Microsoft Store: the app checks its license with the Microsoft Store to manage the free trial.

These services receive only what is listed above, under their own privacy terms. Nothing else leaves your PC.
